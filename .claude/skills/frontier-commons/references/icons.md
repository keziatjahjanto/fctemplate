# Icon library

106 line icons (Lucide, ISC licence) recoloured to the brand. Put a name from the left column in a point's `"icon"`, or leave it out and the builder picks one from the point's title (weighted three times) and text. Use `"icon": "none"` to turn one off, or `"icons": false` on the slide to turn them all off. A slide uses icons for every item or for none.
