# Frontier Commons — Brand Reference

Condensed from *Frontier Commons Branding Guide* (January 2026). Use this as the source of truth for anything visual or written.

## Colours

| Role | Name | HEX | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Onyx** | `#131416` | Primary text, icons, high-contrast elements |
| Primary | **Deep Blue** | `#0D145F` | Logo, headers/headings, secondary section backgrounds |
| Primary | **Mist** | `#F3F4FA` | Backgrounds, content containers/cards, text on dark |
| Secondary | **Soft Periwinkle** | `#9C90E6` | Secondary elements, data, labels, subtle UI accents, section dividers |
| Secondary | **Papaya Whip** | `#FFEFD5` | Soft emphasis blocks, highlight sections, story/community areas |
| Accent | **Amber Gold** | `#FABC20` | Calls to action (buttons, links), key indicators, focus cues — **sparingly** |

Primary colours dominate surfaces and structure. Secondary colours support storytelling and clarity. The accent guides attention and action, so use it once or twice per page or slide, not everywhere.

- Onyx: `#3B3E44` 25% · `#5F646D` 40% · `#848A94` 55% · `#ADB1B8` 70% · `#D6D8DB` 85%
- Deep Blue: `#15219D` 35% · `#1F2FE0` 50% · `#626DEA` 65% · `#A5ACF3` 80% · `#E9EAFC` 95%
- Periwinkle: `#ACA2EA` 78% · `#BCB4EE` 82% · `#CCC6F2` 86% · `#DCD8F6` 91% · `#ECEAFA` 95%
- Amber: `#FBC848` 63% · `#FCD46F` 71% · `#FCE097` 79% · `#FDECBE` 87% · `#FEF8E6` 95%

Tonal steps are fine for infographics, complex interfaces and program-specific themes.

Inter is for display text (headers, headings). DM Sans is for everything you read (section headers, body, captions, buttons). The wordmark is set in a geometric caps face (Montserrat Bold, wide tracking).

## Logo
- **Icon** (three nested "F" strokes): for avatars, favicons, nav bars and footers, internal tools, busy graphics, and places where "Frontier Commons" is already written nearby.
- **Wordmark** (icon + FRONTIER COMMONS, stacked or horizontal): **prefer this for anything external-facing** (presentations, email signatures, partnerships, widely shared promo) while the audience is still getting to know the brand.
- Approved versions: Deep Blue on Mist/white, Mist on Deep Blue, Mist on Onyx.
- **Do:** keep clear space around it (at least the height of one icon bar), make sure the background has enough contrast, and use vector or high-resolution files.
- **Don't:** stretch, squash, rotate, skew, recolour outside the palette, add shadows, outlines or gradients, place it on busy backgrounds, or rearrange the icon bars.

> The logo files in `assets/` are **reconstructions** from the guide. If you have the official vector files, drop them in `assets/` with the same file names.

## Layout signatures
- **Rounded corners** on rectangular elements (cards, photos, buttons are pills).
- **Section dividers:** a Periwinkle background with a thin Mist line across the lower third and a big Mist word sitting on the line, aligned right.
- **Cards:** Papaya Whip rounded cards on white or Mist, with a bold title and a short Amber underline.
- **Tags/pills:** small Amber pill labels above headlines ("Who We Are").
- **Highlights:** in a headline, emphasise the key word in a brighter tone (Blue 50% on light backgrounds, Periwinkle 82% on dark).
- **Photos:** relevant, real photos over polished stock. No heavy filters and nothing pixelated.

## Voice & tone
**Accessible:** simple, everyday words, with no ministry or technical jargon. Write for non-native English speakers, partners new to ministry culture, and people from any background. Share knowledge without intimidating.
**Humble:** no exaggeration, buzzwords, or overly wordy copy. Be culturally aware and don't favour one culture's framework.
**Professional:** clear, polished, purposeful. Every word and visual earns its place. Offer real-world relevance. Forward-thinking.

**Tone:** we *add to* existing work rather than replace it. We help others see new perspectives without being dismissive. We are data-informed but leave room to explore. We are open to conversation and warm and inviting toward collaboration.

**Core principles:** Accessible *over academic* · Collaborative *over competitive* · Innovative *over traditional* · Global *over Western-centric*.

Quick copy check: Would a first-year international student understand this? Is there a buzzword to cut? Does it invite collaboration rather than compete?
